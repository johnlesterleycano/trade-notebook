# How to use Trade Notebook

## First time
1. Open **Settings**. Enter your **trading money** (only money you can afford to lose) and your **max risk per trade** (1% is a good start). For US stocks, enter today's **₱ per $1**.
2. Check the fee rates against your broker's current fee page. Change any that differ.
3. Open **Learn** and work through the roadmap.

## Planning a trade (Calculator)
1. Choose **PH · COL** or **US · Gotrade**.
2. Type the stock, buy price, stop-loss and target. For PH stocks the hint under the price shows the tick size and warns if the price isn't valid on the PSE.
3. **How many shares?** suggests a size that keeps the loss at your stop within your risk limit. Tap **Use … shares**.
4. Read the fee lines, the **break-even sell price** (what the stock must reach before you make anything) and **reward : risk** (aim for 2 : 1 or better).
5. Tap **Log this trade in the journal** when you place the order.

## Journaling
- Every trade needs a **stop-loss** and a **reason** before it saves. Pick a setup and how you feel, too: Review groups results by them.
- Over your risk limit? You'll get a warning and must press **Save anyway**.
- When you sell, open the trade, add the **sell date and price** (and the ₱ per $1 for US trades) and one **lesson**.
- Tick **Practice** for paper trades. Filter Real / Practice in Journal and Review.

## Reviewing (every payday)
- **Expectancy** is the average result per trade after fees. Positive over 30+ trades means your method works.
- **Average R** compares results with planned risk: +2R means you made twice what you risked.
- **Habits to watch** flags losses well past the stop (stop moved or ignored) and trades without a lesson.
- **By how you felt**: if FOMO trades lose, make "no FOMO trades" a rule.

## Your data
Everything stays in this browser. **Settings › Download backup** saves a file. Keep it private: it's your trading history. **Restore backup** on another device replaces what's there.

Keys on a computer: **1–5** switch sections.
