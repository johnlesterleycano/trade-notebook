# Learning roadmap: from zero to your first trades

This is the path the app follows. It's for learning, not financial advice. Go at your own pace; the Learn section lets you tick each item off.

## 1. Foundation first (weeks 0–2)
- Keep **3–6 months of expenses** in an emergency fund before investing.
- Pay off credit cards and other high-interest debt. No investment reliably beats 24%+ interest.
- Only invest money you won't need for **5+ years**.
- Pick a small **"tuition" amount** for trading that you can afford to lose completely. Most beginners lose money at first.

## 2. Market basics (weeks 1–4)
- **Stock**: a small piece of a company. **Index**: a basket that tracks a market (PSEi = 30 big PH companies; S&P 500 = 500 big US companies). **ETF**: a fund you buy like a stock that holds a whole index (FMETF in the PH, VOO or VTI in the US). **Dividend**: cash a company pays its shareholders.
- **Board lots and ticks (PSE)**: you buy in fixed lots (e.g. 100 shares for stocks priced ₱5–₱49.95), and prices move in fixed steps (e.g. ₱0.05 between ₱20 and ₱49.95). The Calculator shows both.
- **Order types**: *limit* (buy at this price or better: use this one), *market* (buy now at any price), *stop* (sell if the price falls to X).
- **Settlement**: T+2, so cash from a sale is usable two trading days later.
- **Fees**: a buy and a sell on the PSE cost about 0.7% together. A stock must rise about that much before you break even.

## 3. Open your accounts
- **COL Financial** for Philippine stocks. Look at its *Easy Investment Program* to invest a fixed amount regularly.
- **Gotrade** for US stocks, with fractional shares (buy $10 of a $500 stock). Fill in the **W-8BEN** form so US dividends are taxed at the 25% treaty rate.
- Compare Gotrade's peso–dollar rate with the market rate before funding: the conversion often costs more than the commission.

## 4. Invest first (months 1–3)
- Pick a fixed amount for every **15th and 30th** payday.
- Buy an index ETF or blue chips with it, whatever the price that day (peso-cost averaging).
- Don't check prices every hour. Notice how a 5% drop feels with real money in.

## 5. Learn to analyse (ongoing)
- **Fundamentals**: is it a good business at a fair price? P/E ratio, dividend yield, debt, earnings growth. Read one company's annual report end to end.
- **Technicals**: when to buy or sell. Support and resistance, trend, moving averages, volume.
- Free sources: COL Financial seminars and research, PSE Academy, Investopedia.

## 6. Practice, then trade small (months 3+)
- Log **10+ practice trades** first (tick "Practice" in the Journal). Write the full plan *before* each: entry, stop-loss, target, reason.
- **Risk at most 1%** of your trading money per trade. The Calculator works out the share count.
- Start real trades small. Journal every one, including how you felt.
- Review monthly: win rate, average win vs average loss, expectancy.
- Add money only after **30+ trades with positive expectancy**.

## Three rules
1. Never trade money you need for bills.
2. Every trade has a stop-loss before you buy, and you don't move it down.
3. If you can't explain why you're buying, don't.
